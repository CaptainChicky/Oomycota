**Oomycota** is a fully static, self-hosted music player that runs from a folder of HTML + MP3 files with no server, no accounts, and no ads. It's designed as a personal alternative to SoundCloud that works offline.

**File structure:**
```
your-site/
  index.html            the player
  tracks.json           track and playlist data (generated by the manager)
  manifest.json         PWA manifest (makes it installable)
  sw.js                 service worker (offline caching)
  icon.png              app icon for home screen
  music/                your MP3 files
  covers/               album art images
  oomycota_manager.py   desktop tool for managing tracks.json
```

**Library and playback.** The main screen shows your full track list. Each track displays cover art (or a generated gradient placeholder based on the title), title, artist, album, a favorite button, and duration. Clicking a track starts playback and builds a queue from all visible tracks starting at that position.

**Playlist filtering.** Horizontal chips at the top let you filter the track list: All, Favorites (auto-generated when you have any), and any custom playlists defined in tracks.json. Selecting a chip filters the view and scopes the queue. Playlists show their cover art image if one was assigned through the manager, falling back to the emoji icon.

**Favorites.** Heart button on every track, in the full player, and in the context menu. Stored in localStorage, persists across sessions. Automatically creates a Favorites filter chip when you have at least one.

**Queue system.** The queue is a slide-up panel accessed from the queue button on the player bar. It shows the current track with playback controls (play/pause, skip, progress bar) at the top, then "Up Next" with the real play order and per-track remove buttons, then "History" of already-played tracks greyed out. There's a "Clear" button to empty the queue. Right-click (desktop) or long-press (mobile) any track anywhere for a context menu with "Play Next" (inserts at front of Up Next) and "Add to Queue" (appends to end).

**Shuffle.** Toggling shuffle physically reorders the Up Next list using Fisher-Yates. Open the queue panel and you see the shuffled order. Toggle off and the original order restores, minus anything that already played. No ghost duplicates.

**Repeat.** Three modes cycling through: off, repeat all (loops the full queue), repeat one (loops current track).

**Player bar.** Persistent bottom bar with cover art, title/artist, prev/play/next controls, time display, shuffle/repeat/queue buttons, and volume slider (desktop). A thin progress bar sits on the top edge and is scrubbable by click or touch-drag.

**Full player.** Tap the cover art or track info on the player bar to open a full-screen overlay with large album art, track info, a scrubable progress bar with timestamps, full playback controls, and favorite + queue buttons. Swipe down to close.

**Search.** Inline search bar in the header on desktop, full-screen overlay on mobile. Searches title, artist, and album fields.

**Media Session.** Lock screen and notification controls on Android and iOS: play/pause, skip forward/back, seek, and a progress bar. Shows track title, artist, album, and cover art. Works with headphone buttons and car Bluetooth controls.

**Offline and PWA.** Installable as a standalone app via "Add to Home Screen." The service worker caches the HTML shell and tracks.json on install, then caches each MP3 and cover image on first play. The download button bulk-caches based on what you're viewing: all tracks, just favorites, or a specific playlist. Long-press the download button to clear the cache and start fresh. Persistent storage is requested on load so the browser won't auto-evict your cached music. After caching, a toast shows storage usage (e.g. "487MB used of 6144MB available").

**Session resume.** Saves current track, playback position, volume, queue state (Up Next + History), shuffle/repeat modes, and active filter to localStorage every 5 seconds and on pause/tab close. Reopen the page and everything is restored. Doesn't auto-play, just cues up where you left off.

**Keyboard shortcuts.** Space (play/pause), left/right arrows (skip), S (shuffle), R (repeat), Escape (close overlays).

**oomycota_manager.py** is a tkinter desktop GUI for managing tracks.json without editing JSON by hand. It scans a `music/` subfolder for MP3 files, reads ID3 tags via mutagen (title, artist, album), extracts embedded album art and saves it to `covers/`, lets you edit track metadata, reorder tracks, assign cover images, create and manage playlists with emoji icons or cover art, and exports tracks.json to the site root. Requires `pip install mutagen` for tag reading.
